Bots that mine, craft, and walk for you. Bots that mine, craft, and walk for you.

CarpetMod+ Beta is a Fabric add-on for Carpet. You spawn a fake player and tell them what to do. If the block is too high to reach, they build up to it. If they don't have any blocks, they go mine some dirt or stone first. Ghosts come from /ghost. A normal /player spawn stays a regular bot.

  • Minecraft 1.21.11
  • Fabric

carpetplus

/player Alex mine diamond_ore continuous
/player Alex walk 120 64 -40
/player Alex attack 5s once
/view Alex

What it can do

Stuff the bots can do.

Mining

They pillar up to reach high blocks.

If they're standing close enough, the block breaks right away. What drops still depends on the tool in their hand. When the ore is above them, they build up to it instead of jumping and swinging. If they have nothing to place, they mine some stone, dirt, or cobble nearby, pick it up, and then pillar.

Craft and smelt

They craft and smelt, then pack up.

You can type diamond or minecraft:diamond. A one-time job runs the whole recipe right then. If the bot placed the crafting table or furnace, they break it when they're done. Smelting still uses fuel.

Enchant and anvil

Enchanting tables and anvils work too.

Enchanting costs lapis and levels. If the bot placed the table, they break it after a one-time enchant. The anvil uses the item they're holding, plus a book or another item. In creative they skip the XP cost, and they still use the materials.

Pathfinding

They can get around walls, lava, and drops.

PathFind starts off on a new world, and the world remembers if you turn it on. With it on, walk-to-coordinates goes around blocks, bridges lava, and hops down short drops. walk ~ ~ ~ follows that player, sprints like a player, climbs out of small holes, and finishes a pillar before chasing. It does not walk into holes. Move forward only builds up over a wall. A schematic walks straight to the next block and pillars. In creative, walking flies.

Ghosts

A ghost that can replay what you did.

/ghost spawns a ghost for a player. Nobody can push them, and they don't shove other players. You can record someone, then play that back on the ghost. When a ghost leaves, chat says they have been removed. /player Steve spawn in survival is a normal bot.

Inventory and skins

Check their inventory, or change the skin.

invsee lets you put items in the hotbar, armor, and offhand. For the skin, copy another player, or fill the whole thing with a color like black, red, or blue. Armor shows right away, and spectator can still go through walls.

Boats and items

They can row a boat, and items can have cooldowns.

In a boat, move forward, back, left, and right rows it, slower than a player would, on land and on water. On the ground, those same commands just walk. /item give gives an item that goes on cooldown when it's used, like an ender pearl.

World settings

Each world keeps its own settings.

With StunShield on, disabling someone's shield pops them up about a block. Team protection keeps teammates from hurting each other. PathFind and StunShield both stay saved on that world.

For Minecraft 1.21.11.

You'll need Fabric Loader and Fabric API. This jar already includes Carpet and Modify Player Data. Voice chat is optional, and the voice-word check uses it. When this beta turns off, the working mod is at modrinth.com/mod/carpetplus. This file is Carpet+BetaV2.17.
